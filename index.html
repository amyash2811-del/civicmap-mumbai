BMC OFFICER DASHBOARD · DEMO

Review, assign and update civic complaints

New complaints are routed here automatically after a short simulated processing delay. The officer can verify the complaint, give field instructions, set an expected completion date, start work and mark it completed.

1. Citizen reports→2. BMC receives→3. Officer verifies→4. Instruction + deadline→5. Work→6. Citizen verifies
